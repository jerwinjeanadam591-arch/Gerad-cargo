Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to information you choose to provide when using the Gerald Cargo website. The website is published at geraldtransporters.co.tz; Gerald Cargo is the business name.

Information you choose to provide

Quote forms may ask for your name, email address, phone number, collection and delivery locations, cargo weight, preferred date, service type, and enquiry details. Please provide only information needed to discuss your transport or construction request. Do not include passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive personal details.

WhatsApp and email enquiries

Submitting a quote form prepares an enquiry and opens WhatsApp. The form does not send the message by itself: review the draft and choose whether to send it in WhatsApp. If you contact us by email, your email provider and Gerald Cargo’s email service will process that message. WhatsApp and email are third-party services with their own privacy terms.

Website hosting, maps, and social links

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. The contact page embeds Google Maps, and the site links to external social networks. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to their services, and they handle it under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently include an analytics or advertising-tracking service. Embedded maps and external services may use their own cookies or similar technologies when loaded.

Access, correction, and enquiries

For questions about an enquiry you have sent, or to request correction or deletion where applicable, contact Gerald Cargo at info@geraldcargo.co.tz. Information already sent through WhatsApp or email may also need to be managed through those services.

Policy updates

We may update this notice when the website or enquiry process changes. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
