Building Construction

Building dependable spaces for homes and businesses

Gerald Cargo extends its practical, coordination-led approach into building construction. We work with clients to understand the intended use, site conditions, scope, and schedule, then coordinate the planning and delivery of suitable project stages. From new residential and commercial buildings to renovations and extensions, our focus is on clear communication, organized work, and thoughtful coordination of materials and site requirements. Each project is discussed individually so the proposed work, responsibilities, timeline, and costs are understood before proceeding.
